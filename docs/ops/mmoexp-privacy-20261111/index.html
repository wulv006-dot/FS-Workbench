Privacy policy for the MMOEXP community safety and onboarding bot.

This policy covers MMOEXP_BOT in the MMOEXP Official Club and MMOEXP D4 VIP Discord servers. The bot is operated by the MMOEXP community administration team. It does not cover the MMOEXP website, purchases, other bots, or Discord’s own services.

To manage your data

Use /privacy in a server where this bot is installed. Choose “Delete my stored data” and confirm privately. If you cannot access the server or bot, contact the team using the public support page below.

Contact MMOEXP support

1. Data we use

  • Discord user and server identifiers, usernames, display names, server nicknames and relevant role information for impersonation checks and configured staff exemptions.
  • Moderation records containing the affected account, the matched name or rule, event time and action or result.
  • Welcome delivery identifiers, join timestamps, delivery status and bot message identifiers to prevent duplicate welcome direct messages. Legacy welcome opening records may remain until their retention period ends.
  • Server and role configuration, allowlists, and limited diagnostic records needed to operate the bot.

Names and role data are evaluated when members join or update their profiles, or when an authorized administrator starts a scan. New members of the Official Club receive one onboarding welcome direct message after the configured safety check. If Discord blocks delivery, the bot records the failure without posting a public replacement or repeatedly retrying. The welcome channel keeps a general guide without identifying new members. Game role menus and privacy replies are private interaction responses. Game choices are applied to Discord roles when you save them; we do not keep a separate history of those choices.

The bot does not request Presence or Message Content privileged access. It does not monitor general chat content or track your online status. Messages you send directly to the bot can be processed to answer your request; they are not used for advertising or AI training.

2. Why we use this data

We use data to identify accounts matching configured impersonation rules, recognize approved staff, respect moderation permissions, welcome new members, process voluntary game role choices and respond to privacy or support requests. Data is not sold, licensed to advertisers, used to profile your relationships or used to train machine learning or AI models.

3. Storage and security

Bot data files and operational backup copies on the bot host are encrypted with AES 256 GCM, which authenticates stored data as well as encrypting it. Encryption keys are kept outside the bot data directory with restricted file access. Bot data is decrypted in memory as needed for operation. The host’s administrative access is restricted.

New system log output from the bot uses generic status labels. Detailed bot diagnostics are written to encrypted files. Preupgrade bot diagnostic entries recovered during migration are handled as encrypted diagnostic records. No security measure can eliminate every risk.

4. Retention

  • Moderation and welcome operational records: up to 30 days, with an hourly cleanup check and another check when the bot starts.
  • Detailed diagnostics, including migrated bot system events: up to 7 days, using the same cleanup schedule.
  • Operational data snapshots in the bot directory: up to 30 days from their last modification.
  • Necessary active configuration, allowlisted account or role identifiers, and identifiers for the bot’s active panels: while configured and necessary for the service.

Cleanup can run up to one hour after a retention period ends while the bot is running. If the bot is offline, cleanup runs before it resumes serving users. We do not claim that every API identifier is deleted within 30 days because necessary active configuration can remain longer.

5. Delete or correct your data

  1. Run /privacy or /privacy-delete in Discord.
  2. Read the private confirmation. Deletion removes stored records and user ID exemptions associated with your own authenticated Discord account.
  3. Confirm deletion. The bot checks its data files and operational backup copies, removes matching records and reports whether deletion was verified.

Confirmation expires after ten minutes and is tied to the account that requested it. You cannot use this flow to delete another person’s data. If a deletion cannot be verified, the bot reports that it is incomplete rather than claiming success.

You can also send the single word privacy directly to the bot for instructions. For access, correction, deletion, complaints, inaccessible accounts or manual support exports, contact the administrators through the existing support channel or the public MMOEXP contact page. State that your request concerns MMOEXP_BOT and provide your Discord user ID. We may ask you to verify account ownership. We do not need your password, login token, payment or government ID for this bot’s self service deletion.

Deleting bot records does not remove your Discord roles, reverse a ban, delete Discord audit logs, or automatically erase previously posted welcome and moderation messages. Contact administrators for those platform actions. Discord manages those records under its own policies. New interactions, rejoining a server or new safety events can generate new records needed for the stated functions.

6. Sharing and service providers

Discord receives API requests needed to operate the bot. Authorized server administrators can see moderation information in restricted Discord log channels. Hosting and infrastructure providers process data only as needed to provide the service, and information may be disclosed when legally required. We do not send member records to the public policy or evidence website.

This static policy website has no custom analytics or advertising code. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web request information under its own service terms. Only public bot interfaces and fabricated test results appear in the review materials.

7. Contact, incidents and changes

Report privacy concerns, security issues or bot misuse to the MMOEXP community administrators through official support. We review reports and investigate appropriate corrective action. If an incident requires notice to Discord or affected users, the operator will provide that notice as required by applicable rules.

This policy will be updated when the bot’s data practices change. The effective date appears at the top. It does not override applicable law or Discord’s Developer Terms.
