## 一、运营中文指南

### 1. 这次采用什么购买规则？

三张卡都能揭晓、查看商品和折扣；同一购物车一次只使用一款活动优惠，四款游戏共用这个规则。只有加购确认成功，才将当前购物车视为已选择该优惠。其余货架暂不可购买，但仍可浏览、揭晓。

这不是每人每日限购。用户完成当前购物车、或自行删除其中商品并确认空车后，可以当天继续购买同一商品、其他货架或其他游戏。

### 2. 为什么不能直接连续买不同货架？

主站购物车只有一个活动折扣码输入位。不同货架可能对应不同折扣档位、游戏和适用范围，后一个码会替换前一个码。活动页因此采用一车一款优惠，避免购物车里已有商品失去原优惠。即使两张卡碰巧使用同一个码，当前规则也要求分开购物车处理。

不新增多码叠加，不改原有优惠适用范围，也不擅自删除、拆单或支付购物车商品。

### 3. 标准购买步骤

1. 从 `https://www.mmoexp.com/events/seasonal/` 打开活动，选择游戏。
2. 按需揭晓固定卡和两张随机卡，比较商品内容及折扣。
3. 选一款优惠，填写页面要求的平台、区域、区服、规格和数量。
4. 点击 **Go to cart**。页面先检查购物车及当前活动配置，再执行必要加购。
5. 主站购物车自动接收并应用对应活动码。核对商品、数量、区服、优惠金额、支付手续费及总额。
6. 用户在主站填写订单和配送资料，按主站流程付款。活动页不能代替主站确认支付成功。

### 4. 返回活动页后会怎样？

- 原商品仍在购物车：当前优惠可查看，其余活动货架暂不可购买；再次打开原优惠不会重复加购。
- 用户明确删除了已确认加购的商品，购物车已空：同一优惠可以重新购买，也可改选其他货架或游戏。
- 页面状态未及时更新：点击 **Check cart status**；返回页面、刷新及同一浏览器其他标签页也会重新核对已有活动购物车。
- 购物车含来源不明或其他商品：先去主站处理当前购物车，不覆盖优惠码、不自动清车。

### 5. 用户报告的两个问题怎样处理？

**场景A：第一货架有优惠，返回后买第二货架导致第一项失去优惠。** 新规则会在第二次加购和换码之前阻止冲突；先完成当前购物车，或由用户自行移除原商品后重新核空车，再选其他优惠。

**场景B：加入一个货架，删车后返回原货架，页面一直处理中。** 新流程不把历史成功记录当成当前购物车内容。确认原项已经删除后，由这次明确点击创建新的加购操作；按钮在成功、失败或检查结束后恢复。不能靠删除历史操作记录来强行重发未知请求。

### 6. 同一款优惠能买几次？

活动页不设每日成交次数限制。只要仍在活动期内、商品及对应规格可用、原码符合主站规则，就可在处理完当前购物车后继续购买。商品库存、主站校验和优惠码有效期仍以当时结果为准。

### 7. “加购被拒绝”和“结果未知”有什么区别？

**明确拒绝**说明该次加购没有成功，可核对选项后重新点击。**结果未知**可能是请求超时或响应丢失，不能判定服务器没有执行；页面不会自动再发一次。

若读取到与原请求一致的商品、数量及套餐组，可按已有商品继续进入购物车，不重复加购。若结果仍未知，即使当前空车，也不能把它当成“原请求绝不会稍后完成”的证明；先检查购物车，必要时由客服核实原操作。

### 8. 取消、刷新、后退会不会自动再加一件？

关闭尚未提交的选项窗口不会加购。点击 Go to cart 后关闭或离开页面，不等于取消已发出的原站请求。后退、前进、刷新及重新打开标签页只恢复检查状态，不因为缺少待用优惠码或看到旧成功记录就重发加购。

不要让用户通过清空浏览器数据或反复点击来处理未知结果；这会丢失本机恢复线索。活动的浏览器记录用于防重复和恢复，不是可靠的跨账号“每个人”身份。

### 9. 平台、区服和数量怎样核对？

只使用页面当次提供且有真实商品映射的选项；缺失的组合不能用其他平台或服务器代替。比如 D4 当前已验组合是 Global / Season 15 Softcore，不能把它写成 Standard。

数量以实际商品单位和套餐内容为准。`1 item` 可能是一整包，商品名里的 `*100` 表示包内容；金币套餐在购物车显示的包数也不能直接当成 K/M 数量。FC 1000K、Aion Kinah、Maple Mesos 和 D4 Gold 不能互相套用单位。

### 10. 页面价格和购物车总额为何可能不同？

活动页显示当次选项的参考价及活动折扣；原站确认实际价格、资格和最终舍入。商品原有折扣可能已经体现在基价中，不能统一假定只乘一次折扣。支付手续费等应按主站实际选择单独核对，不能把参考商品价当含手续费总额。

### 11. 优惠码需要手填吗？能否叠加优惠券、会员积分？

正常活动链路会自动将对应码带入同标签主站购物车，并由主站应用。活动码不与其他优惠券或会员积分叠加。不要手动换成另一货架的码来混合结算。

若自动应用失败，保留原购物车和实际响应，检查码的启用、有效期、站点、游戏、类目及区服范围。不要因失败重复创建同名码，也不要把码已传入误写成已核销或已付款。

### 12. 翻牌何时刷新？为什么仍可能出现相同商品？

每天按 **America/New_York 的自然日**刷新抽取身份和优惠；同一天的有效抽取在返回、刷新及新标签页中复用。商品来自当前已发布候选池，每日抽取不等于每日自动换一份候选名单；不同天也可能抽到同一商品。

每天翻牌刷新不会自动删除购物车商品、清空未知操作或授予新的支付资格。当前购物车仍需按一车一优惠规则处理。

### 13. 活动何时结束？

美东 **2026年11月2日23:59:59（EST，UTC−05:00）**，对应 UTC **11月3日04:59:59**、北京时间 **11月3日12:59:59**。页面倒计时、SEO和正式配置应指向同一时刻。

### 14. 运营和客服怎样记录异常？

记录出现时间、游戏、卡位、商品和规格、数量、页面提示、是否在车中、优惠码及脱敏截图。不要收集用户密码、完整支付凭据或验证码。遇到未知结果先核原操作，不通过换编号、换码或再次加购来“试一下”。

### 15. 哪些追踪已经验证，哪些没有？

此前正式版已实测 D4 8%/10%/15% 自动带码至付款信息页，HWD415 的原站响应与金额另有证据；标记事件的商品、游戏、折扣、码和确认加购可在 FS 专项表核对。

本次购买状态修复须以最终发布后的回归记录为准，不能用旧版通过代替。FS 的“交接码”和“确认加购”不代表券核销、订单或付款。真实付款、退款及收入归因尚无权威支付回传验收。

### 16. 翻牌记录是否需要一直保存？

不建议长期保留每份完整展示内容，但不能因前端每天换 UUID 就直接删除后台旧文件：被删除的旧 UUID 如果再次提交，可能被误当成一次新抽取。UNKNOWN、未决操作和购买恢复线索也不能混进普通清理。

容量阶段先给只读清理计划及可恢复归档方案，核清服务端日期有效性，再考虑对过期普通终态回执保留短期全量、长期仅保留拒绝重放所需的最小记录。现有生产记录不在未批准情况下永久删除；隔离并发结果不当作生产千并发承诺。

## Two — Customer guide and FAQ (English)

### How to shop

1. Open the Halloween Mystery Store and choose FC27, Aion2, Diablo IV or MapleStory.
2. Reveal the fixed pick and both mystery picks if you want to compare all three offers.
3. Choose one offer and select every required platform, region, server, specification and quantity.
4. Select **Go to cart**. The event checks your current cart before sending an add request.
5. In the main store, check the product, quantity, server, event discount, payment fee and final total.
6. Complete your delivery and payment details through the main store's checkout.

### Can I reveal all three cards?

Yes. Revealing or browsing a card does not commit you to purchasing it. You can compare all three offers before choosing one.

### Can I buy more than one offer during the event?

Yes. This event page does not impose a daily purchase limit. Use **one event offer per cart, across all games**. Once you have finished that checkout or removed its items yourself and the cart is confirmed empty, you can choose another offer and purchase again.

### Why are the other offers temporarily unavailable?

Your current cart already contains the selected event offer, or an earlier add is still being checked. The main store uses one discount code per cart, so adding a different offer could replace your existing discount. You can still browse and reveal cards.

### Can I combine two offers if their discounts look the same?

Use separate checkouts. The event follows one offer per cart even if two cards display the same percentage or happen to use the same code. Discounts with the same percentage can still have different eligible products, games or servers.

### What happens when I return to the event page?

If your confirmed item is still in the cart, the selected offer remains available for review and the other offers stay unavailable for purchase. Opening the same confirmed item takes you back to the cart without adding another copy.

### I deleted the item. Can I buy it again?

Yes. Reopen the offer and select its options again. The event checks whether the previous item has actually been removed before creating a new add request. A previous successful add does not use up your offer.

### How do I switch to another offer or game?

Complete your current checkout, or remove the current items yourself in the main store. Return to the event and use **Check cart status** if needed. After the cart is confirmed empty, choose any available offer. The event does not automatically delete or move your existing items.

### What if the item is already in the cart with another quantity or specification?

Review the existing item in the main-store cart. It will not be added again automatically. To choose a different event combination, remove the current item yourself and return to the offer after the cart is empty.

### Why is the cart still being checked?

The event needs a current cart response before it can safely add another item. Check your connection and select **Check cart status**. A failed read does not mean the cart is empty, and it does not trigger another add.

### The store rejected my add. May I try again?

If the event confirms that the store rejected the add, check your options and try again. If the result is described as uncertain or unknown, review the cart first rather than submitting another request.

### An add timed out. Why not just send it again?

The store may have received the first request even if its response was lost. The event will not automatically repeat it. If the exact item and quantity can be confirmed in the cart, you can continue with that existing item. If the result stays uncertain and the item is missing, contact support.

### Will closing the dialog, refreshing or using Back add another item?

Closing the options dialog before selecting Go to cart does not add an item. Leaving after a request has started does not necessarily cancel it. Returning, refreshing or using Back and Forward restores checks; it does not deliberately repeat an uncertain add. Do not clear browser data as a way to retry an unknown request.

### Which platform and server should I select?

Choose the exact options shown for your account and intended delivery. Do not substitute a different platform, realm or season. Only combinations available in the event's verified product options can proceed. Check the main-store cart before paying.

### Does a quantity of “1” always mean one individual unit?

No. It can mean one package. Read the product title, package contents and quantity unit. For example, a product marked “*100” may contain 100 units in one package. Do not treat a package count as a K/M currency amount.

### Why can the final total differ from the event's reference price?

The event displays a reference for the selected combination. The main store confirms the current price, eligible discount and rounding. Payment fees or other charges shown by the main store are separate from the reference product price. Always review the final total there.

### Do I need to enter a code manually?

The normal event flow carries the appropriate code into the main-store cart and attempts to apply it automatically. Check that the cart shows the expected discount before paying. Do not replace it with a code from a different offer to combine purchases.

### Can I combine the event code with a coupon or member points?

No. Event codes cannot be combined with other coupons or member points. The main store makes the final eligibility decision.

### When do the mystery picks refresh?

Picks and discounts reset at midnight in **America/New_York**. Your valid draw is reused during that day's browsing session, including returns and other tabs in the same browser. A daily draw uses the published product pool; it does not guarantee that every day's products will be different.

### Does the daily reset empty my cart or prove that I have paid?

No. A daily reset does not remove your items or resolve a previous uncertain request. An empty cart is also not proof of payment. Use the main store's order and payment confirmation to check your purchase.

### When does the event end?

The Halloween Mystery Store ends on **November 2, 2026 at 23:59:59 US Eastern time (EST, UTC−05:00)**. That is **November 3 at 04:59:59 UTC**. Offers must still be available and eligible when confirmed by the store.

### Can I decline optional analytics?

Yes. Your anonymous analytics choice does not change your offers or purchasing eligibility. The event also uses browser records to preserve draws and help prevent duplicate cart requests; these records are not a cross-account identity or a per-person daily purchase limit.

### Where should I report a problem?

Contact MMOEXP support with the time, game, offer, selected options, quantity and the message you saw. A screenshot with personal information hidden can help. Never send your password, payment credentials or verification codes. Do not submit payment again merely to recover an event-page error.
