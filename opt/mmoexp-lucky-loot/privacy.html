What this bot does

Lucky Loot runs free community activities in configured channels, records entries, draws winners and delivers configured rewards. No purchase or paid entry is required. Each eligible member receives one entry. Activity-specific roles, dates, participation requirements, rewards, quantities and claim deadlines are shown in each activity message. Extra messages do not increase your odds.

Information used

The bot checks your Discord ID, account age, server membership age and roles to enforce activity eligibility. It records participation counts, necessary message identifiers and times, winners and reward status. It does not store chat text or collect presence. Keyword activities may ask you to mention the bot; a short-lived message digest can be used to ignore duplicate entries. Ordinary chat participation uses message metadata only.

Only winners are asked to submit an email through a private form. The email is sent to MMOEXP’s coupon system to grant the selected coupon to the corresponding account. It is not posted in the Discord channel. Do not submit passwords, payment details or someone else’s email. Coupon game restrictions, validity and per-account limits still apply. A pending registration, delivery failure or uncertain result is not a confirmed delivery.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local activity data is encrypted. Activity records, including reward emails and Discord identifiers, are removed within 30 days after the claim deadline, with an hourly cleanup delay. Activity duration and claim periods are each capped at 30 days, so the maximum is 90 days from activity start plus up to one hour. Diagnostic event records are kept for at most 7 days. Configuration needed for an active service remains while in use.

Use /privacy for an in-app explanation and /privacy-delete for a private confirmation to remove your local Lucky Loot records and withdraw your entries. An in-progress or uncertain delivery may require a moderator to reconcile the original operation first; the bot will report that deletion is incomplete. Deletion does not undo delivered coupons, change your MMOEXP account or remove Discord messages. Contact a server administrator for help with those separate records. Re-entering a later activity creates new necessary records.

Recipients and controls

Discord processes platform messages and interactions. The community team’s restricted server hosts the bot, and authorized administrators manage activities. Only the winner’s submitted email and selected coupon information are sent to MMOEXP for reward delivery. Data is not sold, used for advertising profiles or used to train AI models. Token and encryption keys are kept separately from activity data.

Activity integrity

Eligibility is checked again before drawing. Winners are selected randomly without replacement. If fewer eligible members enter than the configured reward quantity, only available eligible members can win. No winner list is silently rerolled. A timeout or uncertain reward response is held for review and is not automatically issued a second time. Administrators may cancel an activity before the draw; cancelled activities do not draw winners. Contact the organizers if an advertised reward cannot be fulfilled.
