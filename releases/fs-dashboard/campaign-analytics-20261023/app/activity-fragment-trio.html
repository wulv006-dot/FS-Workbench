Know your upgrade

Can I include SBC completion with my companions?

Yes. Select a listed SBC player and choose “Include SBC completion”. Your list shows the service and both companions separately. Existing SBC scope and resource requirements apply; contact support first for partially completed challenges.

Which player version will I receive?

Your list specifies the name, rating, version and platform. The featured trio uses actual Star Performer versions. Pairing ideas do not guarantee full chemistry; confirm positions and chemistry in-game.

Will I pay for a core player I already own?

Choose “I already own this player”. The core appears for context only and is excluded from the purchase and total. You buy your selected companions.
