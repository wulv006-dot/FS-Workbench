About the Halloween Mystery Store

A limited-time MMOEXP event with daily-changing offers on FC27, Aion2, World of Warcraft and MapleStory. Flip a card to see what you get.

How do the mystery picks work?

Choose your game, flip your fixed pick and two mystery picks, then select your server. Every offer shows its discount and reference price before you buy.

When do offers refresh?

Picks and discounts reset every day at midnight US Eastern time. Your cards stay the same all day, and tomorrow brings a new draw.

Do I need a discount code?

No. The event discount is applied automatically in your cart. Event offers cannot be combined with other coupons or member points.

When does the event end?

The Halloween Mystery Store closes on November 8 at 23:59:59 US Eastern time. Reveal your offers before the timer runs out.
